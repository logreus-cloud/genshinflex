**In short:** Jahoda is a 4★ Anemo support: she heals, gives Elemental Mastery and deals her enemies' elemental damage off-field. She works best in Moonsign teams: without Ascendant Gleam most of her damage disappears. Her healing scales with ATK and her Burst is expensive, so her key stats are ATK and Energy Recharge.

**How she plays:** her Skill, Savvy Strategy: Splitting the Spoils, is a dash toward an enemy, after which Jahoda zips around the field. If nearby enemies have Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo, she fills her Purr-loined Treasure Flask with that element and then empties it in an Anemo hit. With Ascendant Gleam a full flask isn't emptied at once: Jahoda gradually spends it and fires Fluffy Meowballs that deal elemental damage. Her Burst, Hidden Aces: Seven Tools of the Hunter, summons two assistant robots that heal and attack enemies.

**Team composition:** her passive buffs the robots based on the most common element in the party: with Pyro they hit harder, with Hydro they heal more, with Electro there are three of them, with Cryo they attack more often. When the robots heal an active character above 70% HP, that character gets +100 Elemental Mastery. Jahoda herself raises the party's Moonsign level by 1.

**Stats:** Energy Recharge 180–200%, ATK 1800+, Elemental Mastery around 200.

**Partners:** her best teams are Lunar-Bloom with Nefer and Lauma and Lunar-Charged with Flins, Clorinde, Aino and Ineffa. In regular teams she works as a Swirl healer, for example in an Arlecchino team.

**Constellations:** C2 lets her track two party elements at once, C4 restores energy when the robots change element. C6 is the best: after her flask fills, Moonsign characters get +5% Crit Rate and +40% Crit DMG.

**Weaknesses:** nearly everything strong in her kit depends on Ascendant Gleam. Filling the flask takes field time, and knockback interrupts her Skill. She's good at a bit of everything but the best at nothing.

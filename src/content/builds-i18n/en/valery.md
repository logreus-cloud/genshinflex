**In short:** a 7.2 4★ Electro sword user, a shielder and buffer similar to Shenhe or Yun Jin, but for Electro. His Skill creates an ATK-scaling shield and gives Impact Potential. His Charged Attack, Mortar Fire, spends that Potential and grants 15 stacks of Assault Command: each Electro hit from the active character gets bonus damage based on Valeriy's ATK.

**Best synergy: Mitya.** Inside the Polestar Field Valeriy enters Radiance, and the stacks start boosting Stellar-Conduct damage three times as hard: 88% ATK per stack instead of 29%, and 0.9% ATK per Potential point instead of 0.3%. Outside these teams he still works with Electro DPS units, just weaker.

**Stats:** everything into ATK — both the shield and the damage bonus depend on it. Energy Recharge keeps his Burst ready every rotation; without it, only his Skill builds Potential.

**Constellations:** C1 solves energy, C2 gives the party +100 EM (useful for Mitya), C6 doubles Mortar Fire damage and adds crit. A strong constellation line for a 4★, worth collecting.

Skill and multipliers are from the 7.2 beta and may change after release.

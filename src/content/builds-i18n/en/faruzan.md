**In short:** an Anemo support: her Burst grants an Anemo DMG bonus and shreds enemy Anemo resistance. Since 7.1 she's also one of the best buffers for Stellar Swirl — together with Vesna or Mizuki.

**Stats:** Energy Recharge is what matters: 250–300% before C6 and about 200% at C6. With Favonius Warbow you need 50%+ Crit Rate for the weapon to proc. For personal damage — Anemo goblet and a crit circlet.

**Rotation:** Skill → Charged Attack → Burst → switch to the main DPS; Skill again when it's off cooldown.

**Partners:** in Stellar Swirl — Vesna, Odette and Vodyanitsa, or Mizuki with the Cryo Traveler; among classic teams — Xiao and Wanderer.

**Constellations:** C6 is the key one: +40% Crit DMG on Anemo attacks under her buff, and her Skill starts hitting enemies consistently (which is why Tenacity of the Millelith is her best set at C6). C2 extends her Burst by 6 seconds, C4 gives energy.

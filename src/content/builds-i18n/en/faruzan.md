**In short:** Faruzan is a narrow but very strong Anemo support. She doesn't deal much damage herself, but her Burst gives the team an Anemo DMG Bonus and cuts enemies' Anemo RES by 30%. Since 7.1 she's also one of the best buffers for Stellar Swirl — together with Vesna or Mizuki.

**How she plays:** her Burst, The Wind's Secret Ways, places the Dazzling Polyhedron for 12 seconds. It moves along a triangle and releases a Whirlwind Pulse at each corner: the pulse lowers enemies' Anemo RES and gives allies Prayerful Wind's Benefit — an Anemo DMG Bonus (about 32% at talent level 10). While the Benefit is active, an ally's first Anemo hit deals extra damage equal to 32% of Faruzan's Base ATK (her passive Lost Wisdom of the Seven Caverns), once every 0.8 s. Her Skill, Wind Realm of Nasamjnin, charges her next Aimed Shot into a Hurricane Arrow, and its Pressurized Collapse vortex also shreds resistance and pulls enemies in.

**Energy:** Faruzan's main problem. Her Burst costs 80 Energy and her Skill makes few particles, so before C6 she needs a lot of Energy Recharge and a weapon with ER or particle generation.

**Stats:** Energy Recharge — 250–300% before C6 and about 200% at C6. Everything else is secondary: with Favonius Warbow she needs at least 50% Crit Rate so the weapon procs; for personal damage, Anemo goblet and a crit circlet. Talents: Burst first (it raises the Anemo DMG Bonus), then Skill; Normal Attack isn't needed.

**Partners:** in Stellar Swirl — Vesna, Odette and Vodyanitsa, or Mizuki with the Cryo Traveler. Among classic Anemo DPS — Xiao (with Furina and Xianyun) and Wanderer (with Xiangling and Bennett). Outside Anemo teams she's nearly useless.

**Constellations:** C6 is the key one and changes her role: allies under the Benefit get +40% Crit DMG on Anemo attacks, and the active character's hits apply Pressurized Collapse on their own once every 3 s — the vortices start shredding resistance and generating energy consistently. That's why Tenacity of the Millelith is her best set at C6: her Skill always hits. C2 keeps the Polyhedron 6 seconds longer, C4 returns up to 4 Energy per vortex, and C1 gives a second Hurricane Arrow per Skill.

**Weaknesses:** before C6 — serious energy problems and little value outside Anemo teams. Her full potential only unlocks at C6, and her buff only helps characters who deal Anemo damage.

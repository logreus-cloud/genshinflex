**In short:** a 7.3 4★ Electro sword user for Stellar-Conduct. Off-field, her anchor follows up Electro and Cryo hits from the active character with coordinated attacks and builds Excitement points. At full Excitement, Danica comes in with Entrance E2 and becomes the Guardian Maid: a shield on the active character and a party ATK buff from her Burst.

**Why she matters:** enemies around the anchor lose 15% Electro RES, and in Radiance (the Polestar Field) another 15% Electro and 30% Cryo RES. Her passive also raises the party's base Stellar-Conduct DMG from her ATK (0.7% per 100 ATK, up to 14% at 2000 ATK).

**Stats:** everything into ATK — the shield, ATK buff, reaction bonus and constellations all depend on it. Energy Recharge matters until C1: her Burst costs 60.

**Signature sword** (5★, 741 base ATK, 16.5% ATK): +16% ATK; her Electro hits apply Imperial Edict to the enemy for 15s — +20% Stellar-Conduct DMG from the active character. When Danica triggers the reaction or hits with it, the edict strengthens: another +16% ATK for her and +20% reaction damage for the active character. It works off-field too, so it's a buff for the whole team. It will be added to the site's database with 7.3.

**The new 4★ sword from 7.3** (45.9% Energy Recharge) also suits her: after 5s off-field it gives +20% ATK and a 280% ATK strike when she comes on field (once every 14s), and the effect only ends after 10s on field. It fits the "come in with Entrance E2, swap out" playstyle and covers energy before C1.

**Second form:** as the Battle Maid she becomes an on-field DPS with anchor attacks and On-Field E2. It's viable at C6, but until then Danica is stronger as a support.

**Constellations:** C1 solves energy and strengthens both form buffs, C2 (Banquet Toast) sharply raises the whole party's reaction damage, C4 gives energy to the others. The best stopping points are C2 and C4.

Everything is based on leaks: weapons, artifacts and teams are picked from her kit, and multipliers and weapon type may change by release.

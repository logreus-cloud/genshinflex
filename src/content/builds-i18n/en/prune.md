**In short:** Prune is a 4★ Anemo support who attacks off-field and boosts the team's damage. Her buff scales with her own ATK, so her build is almost entirely ATK, and weapons are chosen for high base ATK.

**How she plays:** her Skill, Ring-A-Ding-Ding! Hexhunter Chime, deals Anemo damage. If it triggers Swirl, for 6 seconds it changes so that the Banehunter Oathhammer takes on the swirled element and Prune knocks it into enemies. Her Burst, The Bell Tolls! The Hunt Is On!, summons a bell that follows the active character and deals AoE Anemo damage. When the bell triggers Swirl, a hammer with that element appears next to the enemy.

**The main buff:** after an infused hammer hits, allies get a damage bonus of 0.025% for every point of Prune's ATK above 2000. The higher her ATK, the stronger the buff — so ATK goes on both main stats and substats, and Energy Recharge only as needed.

**Stats:** ATK as high as possible; the buff only starts working above 2000. Get just enough Energy Recharge for her Burst to be ready every rotation.

**Partners:** she shines most in Hexerei teams: Varka and Venti trigger Swirl and get her buff, Nicole adds ATK, Durin attacks off-field. Outside Hexerei Prune works as a regular Anemo sub DPS — for example with Clorinde or Xiao.

**Constellations:** C2 is the recommended stop: Prune gains ATK during her Burst, so the team buff grows. C1 restores energy, C4 makes the hammer bounce. C6 is the best: the bell lasts 4 seconds longer, and allies gain ATK after triggering reactions.

**Weaknesses:** without Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo to swirl, her kit barely works. Her buff is weaker than 5★ supports', and her full potential needs constellations.

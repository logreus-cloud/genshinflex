**In short:** the Cryo Traveler is a Stellar reaction character whose damage scales with ATK. In Stellar-Conduct they're the on-field main DPS; in Stellar Swirl, a sub-DPS providing Cryo for the reaction. The role decides the set and leveling order.

**Set by team:** Stellar-Conduct — Disenchantment in Deep Shadow; Stellar Swirl — Tenacity of the Millelith (ATK buff for the whole team), or Heart of the Furnace if Odette isn't using it.

**How they play:** the Skill builds Frost Radiance stacks and the Burst spends them for boosted damage — that's why the Burst is leveled first. In Stellar-Conduct: Skill → two Normal Attacks and a Charged Attack → Burst at full stacks. In Stellar Swirl: Skill → special Charged Attack → Burst.

**Stats:** ATK 2000+ (Game8 recommends 2400+), Crit Rate 60–70%, Crit DMG 140%+, Energy Recharge 125–145%, Elemental Mastery 150–200 as a bonus.

**Constellations:** C1 helps with energy, C2 gives Elemental Mastery, C6 is the strongest: it boosts the whole team's Stellar Glimmer.

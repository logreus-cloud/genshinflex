**In short:** a 7.2 5★ Electro catalyst and the core of Stellar-Conduct teams (Electro + Cryo). All his damage and buffs come from **Elemental Mastery**; ATK does almost nothing for him.

**Two Skill modes:** tap creates a Steady Core: Mitya leaves the field, the core collects Gradient Beacons, and each beacon gives the party +30 EM (up to +120). Hold creates an Overloaded Core: his Charged Attack becomes Gradient Detonation, which costs no Stamina, spends beacons and deals Stellar-Conduct damage. This is the main DPS mode.

**Stats:** EM on sands and goblet, crit on the circlet. His passive raises the party's base Stellar-Conduct DMG by 0.028% per EM point, capped at 14% at 500 EM. Beacons give him up to +20% CRIT Rate, so he needs less crit in substats than usual.

**Weapons:** in early access tests his signature catalyst gives about +35% damage over Sacrificial Fragments, but it's not on the site yet. The best 4★ is Winter's Heavy Heart.

**Partners:** Odette or Diona as the Cryo for the reaction, Yae Miko as the second Electro, and Valeriy as the buffer: inside Mitya's field he enters Radiance and boosts reaction damage specifically. If you only need an off-field support, you can wait for Danica in 7.3.

Early access data: multipliers and rankings may change after 7.2 goes live.

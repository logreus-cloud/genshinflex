We promised to add the Theater enemies as soon as they were announced — and went further: both endgame pages are now full guides.

### Imaginarium Theater: a guide for every act

The [Theater](/en/theater/) page now covers all of season 28:

- **all five difficulties**, from Easy to Lunar: enemy levels, which acts are included and how many characters you need;
- **12 stages** — ten acts and two Arcana challenges. Each one lists enemies by wave, their weaknesses, arena effects and expandable tips;
- **teams per stage** — who to bring for that specific act, not just for the season.

### Stygian Onslaught: every fight explained

The [Onslaught](/en/onslaught/) page lists all six difficulties, from Normal to Dire, and breaks down the three bosses: their forms on each difficulty, what to hit them with and which mechanics to interrupt. Tips and teams are per fight too.

Both pages now have **video guides** right on the site and a list of further reading.

### Weapons, redesigned

The [weapon list](/en/weapons/) has new tall cards with art and rarity glow. Hover (or tap on mobile) to expand a card and see level 90 stats, the passive, type and version. Sorting works like the character list: Newest, Oldest, By name and By version, and your choice is remembered.

### Artifacts, redesigned

[Artifacts](/en/artifacts/) are updated too. Hover over a set to see who wears it. Click it and the card expands to full width: 2- and 4-piece bonuses, version, number of builds and characters with links to their builds — those who use the set as their main one come first. You can now share a link to a specific set.

### Calendar

The [calendar](/en/calendar/) is easier to browse: hold the left mouse button and drag the timeline in either direction — it keeps scrolling with inertia. You can also pick a bar style: Compact, Line or Expanded.

### Illuga in the simulator

The [team simulator](/en/tools/simulator/) runs the latest gcsim and now supports [Illuga](/en/characters/illuga/) — in a team with Navia, Columbina and Yelan. Illuga also gets an "Is it worth pulling" breakdown.

### Fixes

- The Snezhnaya theme now has a sharp high-resolution background on 2K, 4K and ultrawide monitors — and runs faster.
- [Wish tracker](/en/tools/wishes/): the selected UID (including "Manual input") no longer resets when the account list is empty.
- The wish history link script finds the active link more reliably and gives clearer error messages.
- Profile: if you switch accounts while saving, changes won't end up in the other profile; signing in or out in another tab updates the header right away.

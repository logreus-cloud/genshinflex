We updated the cards in the [character list](/en/characters/). They now look like the wish cards in the game.

### What changed

- The art comes from the official wish cards. The character is cut out of the card and framed in the center, like in the game.
- The background depends on rarity: gold for 5★, purple for 4★, so you can tell rarity at a glance.
- The cards are taller and narrower, so more characters fit in a row on a wide screen.
- Below the name you'll see the in-game element and weapon type icons. The List view uses the in-game weapon icon too.
- Long names shrink to fit the card instead of being cut off.

The Traveler and beta characters don't have a wish card yet, so their art is cut from the splash. Once a character is released, their card updates on its own.

If a card looks off, let us know through [feedback](/en/feedback/).

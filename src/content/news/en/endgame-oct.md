Two endgame modes have rotated at once, and both pages are updated.

### Stygian Onslaught: “Luminous Battlefront”

The 7.1 [Stygian Onslaught](/en/onslaught/) runs from September 30 to November 3. This season has three bosses:

- **Battle-Hardened Domovoy Sculptor (“Churin”)** carves ice sculptures that strike your active character. Hit it with Stellar Glimmer before a sculpture is finished and it will explode, lowering the boss's resistances.
- **Secret Source Automaton: Overseer Device** opens the fight with huge resistance. Frequent Cryo hits drain its momentum and open a damage window, so many small hits beat one big burst here.
- **Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow** raises a Cryo shield at 80% HP. Break it with Electro (Lunar-Charged works) or Pyro, and the boss freezes for 10 seconds with low resistances.

A character can't be used in two fights. Two bosses want Cryo, so build two different Cryo teams in advance (or a Cryo team and a Stellar Glimmer team) and save Electro for the third fight. Recommended teams for each fight are on the mode page.

### Imaginarium Theater: season 28

The [Theater](/en/theater/) runs from October 1 to November 1. Allowed elements are **Hydro, Cryo and Anemo**.

- **Opening characters:** Neuvillette, Barbara, Citlali, Kaeya, Wanderer and Prune. For the whole season they get +20% Max HP, ATK and DEF — both in the Theater and in the open world.
- **Special guest stars:** Ineffa, Yae Miko, Thoma and Chiori. You can bring them even though their element doesn't match, and their stat bonus is doubled.

We put together four teams for the season rules: Neuvillette hypercarry, Cryo Skirk, Wanderer and Electro-Charged with the guest stars. Act enemies haven't been announced yet — we'll add them as soon as they are.

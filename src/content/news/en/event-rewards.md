### What they are

Until now, every profile decoration was open to everyone: pick a frame, an effect or a background and you’re done. Now there are **rewards**: cosmetics you can’t take yourself — they are given for taking part in site events.

There are two kinds of rewards:

- **Avatar frame** — sits next to the regular frames and is marked “Event”.
- **Profile glow** — an animated outline around your profile card that everyone visiting your profile can see.

A reward stays yours for good, and you can turn it on or off any time.

### The first rewards: Crimson Gaze and Crimson Aura

- **Crimson Gaze** — a frame of red rings with notches, like the iris of an eye. It slowly turns, pulses and glitches every now and then.
- **Crimson Aura** — a red glow around the profile card with a highlight running along its edge.

For now, only the site team has these two. More will arrive with the first events — we’ll announce them separately.

### Where to turn them on

Open [your profile](/profile/) → “Customize profile”:

- frame — “Avatar” tab, “Frame” section;
- glow — “Background and effects” tab, “Glow” section.

If you don’t have any rewards yet, you’ll only see the regular options there.

With reduced motion turned on in your system settings, the frame and glow are shown without animation.

### One more thing

The site header now shows your avatar instead of an icon with the first letter of your nickname: your uploaded picture or the character you picked. If you haven’t set an avatar, the old icon stays.

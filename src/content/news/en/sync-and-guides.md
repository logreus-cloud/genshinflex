This update is about convenience: everything of yours now lives in your account, guides are more detailed, and the site works better on phones.

### A new Characters tab

[Characters](/en/characters/) has a fresh look:

- tall cards with character art, framed on the face; leaked characters show their splash art;
- three views: "Gallery" with large cards, a compact "Grid" and a "List";
- hover over a card to preview the build — best weapons and artifacts without opening the page;
- sort by "Newest first", "Oldest first" or "By name"; characters show as one continuous grid by default, and you can group them with the "By version" button.

All lists — characters, weapons and artifacts — now remember your filters, search, sorting and view: open a character, come back, and your selection is still there.

### Your profile on every device

If you're signed in to your [account](/en/account/), ["My profile"](/en/profile/) is no longer tied to one browser. Customization (including uploaded avatar, cover and background), favorites, "my characters" and your showcase UID are saved to your account and show up on your phone, computer or any other browser — just sign in.

Profile customization is part of your public profile: friends will see it once profile pages arrive. Without an account everything works as before and stays in your browser.

### Wish history in your account

In the [wish tracker](/en/tools/wishes/), signed-in users' wish history is now stored in the account instead of the browser and opens on any device. History you've already loaded into the browser moves to your account automatically when you sign in. Without an account the tracker works as before.

### Rotations in guides

Character guides now have a "Rotation" section — the action order in short notation like `E → Q → M1-M2-M3`, with a legend. For now eight characters from the list below have rotations; we'll add the rest gradually.

### Updated guides

The guides for [Vesna](/en/characters/vesna/), [Sandrone](/en/characters/sandrone/), [Odette](/en/characters/odette/), [Alyosha](/en/characters/alyosha/), [Vodyanitsa](/en/characters/vodyanitsa/), [Faruzan](/en/characters/faruzan/), [Yae Miko](/en/characters/yae-miko/) and the [Cryo Traveler](/en/characters/traveler-cryo/) were rewritten from several sources: more weapon options (including 4★ and craftable ones), budget teams, stat targets, kit breakdowns and constellation advice. Faruzan now has a full breakdown covering her new role in Stellar Swirl.

### Mobile

- Profile: the "Account" strip no longer gets squeezed, and long nicknames no longer break mid-word.
- The character list on phones is a third shorter — Gallery cards are more compact.
- The calendar's Timeline view leaves more room for events.
- Banner dots and small buttons are easier to tap on touchscreens.

### Fixes

- The Snezhnaya theme no longer lags on high-resolution, high-refresh-rate monitors.
- [Team Simulator](/en/tools/simulator/): "Stop" now also cancels the first engine download, and the simulator warns you when the showcase has no talent levels.
- [Team checker](/en/tools/team-check/): focus is no longer lost after closing the editor — friendlier for keyboard users.

Our biggest update since launch: a fresh look, a personal profile and optional accounts.

### New design and the Snezhnaya theme

- A new home page: a banner carousel with timers, an "Explore the site" block by section, news and a calendar with pictures, and strips of the newest characters and weapons.
- A theme for patch 7.1 — Snezhnaya: an in-game backdrop with a gentle parallax, snowfall, northern lights and frosty headings. On your first visit you get an entrance screen: touch Vesna's compass.
- A new header and side menu, game-style icons and smooth page transitions. The light theme now uses a bright gold.
- Better on phones: the banner, strips, wish tracker and Abyss floor buttons no longer overflow the screen.

### My profile

[My profile](/en/profile/) gathers everything of yours in one place: favorites, characters from the Team checker or your UID showcase, and a wish summary. You can style it with a cover and background (GIFs work), an avatar frame, any accent color and page effects. Everything stays in your browser, and you can save the profile to a file to move it to another device.

### Background music

The official Snezhnaya soundtrack in a mini player, with volume, mute and track controls. Music keeps playing as you move between pages. It's off by default.

### Accounts

The site still works without signing up, but you can now [create an account](/en/account/register/) with email and password or through Discord, Google or Telegram. For now an account covers sign-in and settings; soon it will sync your profile, roster and wish history across devices.

Our [privacy policy](/en/privacy/) explains what data the site keeps. From your [account](/en/account/) you can download all your data as one file or delete the account.

### Community

- We now have a [Discord server](https://discord.gg/rQ6pTDqdX9): site news, forums for bugs and ideas, and a bot that reminds you about Abyss, Theater and Onslaught resets and about banners and events starting or ending.
- Messages sent through [feedback](/en/feedback/) now reach the team right away.

### Leaked characters (BETA)

⚠️ Spoilers. [Mitya](/en/characters/mitya/) and [Valery](/en/characters/valery/) now have talents and constellations from the leak (multipliers at talent level 6), plus an "Analysis" section marked "AI analysis": role, what to build and who to team them with. Everything may change before release.

### Team simulator

- The Traveler is supported: the element is read from your showcase, and there are reference teams for all seven elements.
- Level 95 and 100 characters are no longer skipped.
- A clear message when showcase data can't be read.

### Fixes

- Tall weapon art (the bell, books) no longer stretches tiles in the weapon list.

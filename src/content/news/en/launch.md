Hello! **GenshinFlex is live.** Over the past 16 hours, we built this project from scratch: from the first pages to a database, guides and tools for everyday play in Genshin Impact.

We wanted to bring together the things that usually take several tabs to find: how to build a character, who to put on a team, what awaits in the Abyss and how many materials an upgrade needs. The site is free, with no ads or registration.

This is the first entry in the project's history. Below is everything included at launch. Future news and changelogs will appear on this same page.

### Home, search and everyday features

- The [home page](/en/) brings together current wish banners, Abyss, Theater and Onslaught timers, new characters and weapons, and shortcuts to tools.
- Search covers names and guide text. Recent pages and favorites help you return to useful content.
- Russian, English and Spanish versions, light and dark themes, and layouts for phones and computers are available.
- Select your game server to use regional timers. Navigation also links to the official interactive map.
- The news banner on the home page opens the relevant entry in the full update history.

### Characters, weapons and artifacts

- A [character database](/en/characters/) with filters, stats by level, talents, their multipliers and constellations.
- A [weapon database](/en/weapons/) with stats, refinement effects from R1 to R5, and acquisition sources including wishes, forging and events.
- An [artifact reference](/en/artifacts/) with set bonuses.
- Character pages include recommended weapons, artifact sets, main stats, substats, talent priorities and sample teams.

### Guides and upgrade choices

- A [guide directory](/en/guides/), written breakdowns, external resources and creators' videos. Sources are linked from the guides.
- Where simulation data is available, character pages include “Worth pulling?”: team damage gains from constellations and weapons using gcsim. Results apply to the stated team and simulation conditions.
- Use the [guide editor](/en/guides/editor/) to suggest a guide or correction. Submissions can cover characters, weapons and endgame content and are reviewed before publication.

### Abyss, Theater and Onslaught

- An [endgame overview](/en/rotation/) and dedicated pages for [Spiral Abyss](/en/abyss/), [Imaginarium Theater](/en/theater/) and [Stygian Onslaught](/en/onslaught/).
- Abyss floors 9–12, enemies by chamber and half, conditions and team recommendations.
- Cycle details, restrictions, buffs, enemies and recommended teams for Theater and Onslaught.
- Cycle dates and timers, with support for community endgame guides tied to a specific cycle.

### Abyss team checker

The [team checker](/en/tools/team-check/) helps you prepare two teams for Floor 12:

- Add characters by name, from a searchable list with element filters, or by UID through a public Enka showcase.
- Edit levels, constellations, talents, weapons, artifacts and final stats.
- Assign characters to either half, use recommended teams or pick teams from your roster automatically.
- Get a build and half-requirement assessment with explanations, updated after edits. This is guidance, not a simulation of an actual run.
- Clear a half, reset both teams or clear the roster with confirmation. Refreshing a showcase by UID keeps characters in their selected teams.

### Wish tracker

The [wish tracker](/en/tools/wishes/) supports importing through an in-game link, UIGF 3/4 files and manual entry. View history, pity counters, 50/50 statistics and Primogem spending, and export your history as UIGF. Repeated imports merge records without duplicates.

### Rotations and leveling

- The [rotation builder](/en/tools/rotation-builder/) lets you choose four characters, weapons, sets and an action sequence. Start with a team from a guide and share the result through a link.
- The [leveling calculator](/en/tools/calculator/) totals character, talent and weapon materials between selected levels, including EXP books, ore and Mora. It includes upgrade presets and shareable calculations.

### Feedback and data

Your roster, wish history, favorites and recent pages are stored in your browser. Showcase and wish-history requests pass through the server to retrieve data from external services. Read about sources and data storage on the [about page](/en/about/).

Use [feedback](/en/feedback/) to report bugs, suggest improvements or correct information. A private moderation panel is in place to handle messages and guide submissions.

### Still to come

[Community and run-based rankings](/en/rating/) are still in development: voting and run submissions are not available yet. We will continue expanding the content and fixing issues as the site grows.

Thanks for being here at the start. Try the tools and tell us what is missing — your feedback will help us choose what to improve next.

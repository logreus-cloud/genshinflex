A lot has piled up on the site over the last few days, so here it all is in one post.

### Full Theater and Onslaught guides

The [Imaginarium Theater](/en/theater/) and [Stygian Onslaught](/en/onslaught/) pages are now full guides:

- **A difficulty table** — enemy levels and what changes at each tier. The Theater has five modes from Easy to Lunar; the Onslaught has six difficulties from Normal to Dire.
- **Every Theater act** (1–10 plus both Arcana Challenges) and **all three Onslaught fights**. Each shows the enemies by part of the battle, the star condition, advantages, stage effects and boss mechanics. For the Onslaught you can also see how the bosses change on higher difficulties.
- **Tips and teams for every stage** — tucked under a “How to clear” button so the page stays tidy.
- **Video guides and links to more guides** — in Russian and English.

We also fixed some data: the Onslaught's official name is “Luminous Battlefront”, and the boss in Theater Act 8 depends on the mode — the Overseer Device on Hard, the Chimeric Winged Lion on Visionary and Lunar.

### Calendar

- You can now drag the [calendar](/en/calendar/) timeline with the mouse: hold the left button anywhere and move in any direction. A short click on an event still opens its card, and nothing changes on phones.
- A new switch for bar styles: **Compact** (thin bars with names), **Line** (colored lines only — a whole patch on one screen) and **Expanded** (as before, with art). Your choice is remembered.

### Weapons and artifacts

- [Weapons](/en/weapons/): new tall cards with large art and a rarity glow. On hover a card expands to show stars, ATK and the substat at level 90, the passive, type and version. Sorting works like on the characters page: newest, oldest, by name or by version.
- [Artifacts](/en/artifacts/): new set cards. Hover to see who wears a set; click to open the 2- and 4-piece bonuses, version and characters with links to their builds.

### Simulator

- The [simulator](/en/tools/simulator/) engine has been updated: **Illuga** is in, with a reference team (Navia, Columbina, Yelan) and an “Is it worth pulling” section.
- The simulator and the wish tracker now fill in the UID from your profile if you haven't entered one yet.

### Profile and account

- Exporting your profile to a file now keeps favorites in all three languages.
- Importing from a file no longer wipes “My characters” progress and no longer leaves half-imported data on errors: it's all or nothing.
- Signing in or out in one tab now shows up in your other tabs right away.
- If you sign in to another account in a different tab, data deletion and export won't apply to it: the page refreshes and asks you to confirm again. Your title, showcase and profile style can no longer end up on the wrong account either.

### Fixes

- The wish tracker no longer resets the selected account while the history is empty.
- The wish history link script no longer trips over itself in two windows, explains a missing cache more clearly and doesn't copy broken links.
- The “Snezhnaya” theme background is no longer blurry on 2K/4K and ultrawide screens, and the winter theme runs more smoothly.
- “Rating” in the footer is dimmed like in the menu: the section is still in progress.

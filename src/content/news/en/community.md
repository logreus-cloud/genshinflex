### Forum

The site now has its own [forum](/en/forum/). Sections: Builds and teams, Endgame, Banners and wishes, Story and lore, Ideas for the site, Chat and Announcements. Everyone can read; posting requires signing in.

- Threads support formatting: **bold**, *italics*, quotes, lists, links and ||spoilers||. You can reply with a quote, react, and edit or delete your own posts.
- **@nickname** becomes a link to the profile, and the mentioned player gets a notification.
- One forum for all three languages, with a filter by thread language.
- Character pages, Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater and Stygian Onslaught now end with a **Discussions** block: the latest threads about them and a "Start a thread about …" button.
- The [community rules](/en/rules/) are in the footer and next to the reply form.

### Follows, friends and feed

On a player's public page (**genshinflex.com/u/name**) you can now **follow**, **add as a friend**, **message** or **block** them.

- Friend requests live on the [Friends](/en/friends/) page, along with your block list.
- The [following feed](/en/feed/) shows new threads and replies from people you follow.
- In Privacy you can hide your follower and friend lists and choose who can send you requests and messages.

### Notifications

The header now has a bell. It shows replies in your threads, mentions, new followers, friend requests and messages as they arrive. The full history is on the [Notifications](/en/notifications/) page.

- Choose which notifications you get in the Notifications tab of the profile editor.
- If you signed in with Telegram, you can also turn on **Telegram forwarding** there. The @GenshinFlex_Bot bot sends the notification with a link; press "Start" in the bot once to allow it.

### Private messages

[Messages](/en/messages/) are one-to-one conversations. New messages arrive without reloading, and the envelope icon in the header shows unread conversations.

- Start a conversation from a player's public page with the Message button.
- You can delete your own messages and report someone else's. A moderator sees only that message and a few around it; moderators don't read conversations otherwise.

### Ranking and guilds

- [Top members](/en/forum/top/) is the forum ranking. You earn points when other players react to your posts, reply in your threads and view your threads. Your points and rank appear on your public page.
- [Guilds](/en/guilds/). Create your own or join an existing one. The guild tag appears next to your name on the forum and in your profile. This is the first version; guilds will grow later.

### Guides and profile

- A full **Faruzan** guide: her burst and skill, energy problems and target Energy Recharge before and after C6, weapons and sets, teams and a constellation breakdown. In all three languages.
- You can now **crop** your own profile images (avatar, cover and background) right in the editor: move and zoom them, including GIFs without losing animation. The size limit is now 50 MB, and backgrounds and covers are stored in high resolution.
- The home page opens immediately, without the intro animation.

### Fixes

- Large images (2K–4K) for backgrounds and covers no longer get stuck while processing.
- Switching accounts on the same device no longer carries one account's profile design and data over to another.

The [privacy policy](/en/privacy/) has been updated to describe what we store for follows, notifications, messages, guilds and the ranking.

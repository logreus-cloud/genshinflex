Halloween is over and the site is back to normal: snow, the usual logo and the Snezhnaya winter look.

### Halloween rewards

If you visited GenshinFlex while signed in on October 31, you keep:

- the “Pumpkin Lantern” avatar frame;
- the “Halloween Night” card glow with bats;
- the “Halloween 2026” title.

They can no longer be earned. You can select them in your [profile](/en/profile/), under appearance settings.

Thanks to everyone who stopped by for the holiday!

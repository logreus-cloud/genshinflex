We've updated the pages of two endgame modes. The Version 7.2 Spiral Abyss starts on November 16 — its page will be replaced on the day it resets.

### Imaginarium Theater: season 29

The [Theater](/en/theater/) runs from November 1 to December 1. The allowed elements are **Hydro, Electro and Geo**.

- **Opening characters:** Yelan, Aino, Flins, Fischl, Zhongli and Kachina. They get +20% Max HP, ATK and DEF.
- **Special guest stars:** Qiqi, Odette, Vesna and Jahoda. You can use them even though Cryo and Anemo are not part of the season.

This season's villains:

- **Act 3, Jadeplume Terrorshroom.** Bring frequent Electro: it fills the Activation gauge, after which the boss becomes exhausted and stays down for a long time.
- **Act 6, Lord of the Hidden Depths.** It raises a shield that Lunar-Charged and Lunar-Crystallize break fastest. Save Flins with Aino or the Zibai team for this one.
- **Act 8, Battle-Hardened Samachurl.** It cooks soup in a pot. Put out the bonfire with Hydro and break the pot with Geo attacks or a claymore.
- **Act 10, Radiant Moonfly.** It applies a Cocoon that only goes away when a character is healed to full HP. Bring a party-wide healer.

The mode page lists the enemies of every act, tips and teams, including both Arcana Challenges of Lunar mode.

### Stygian Onslaught 7.2

The [Onslaught](/en/onslaught/) runs from November 11 to December 15, with the Disturbance Outbreak lasting until November 21. There are three bosses:

- **Wavebinder** drinks from a flask and enters the Valiant state with a shield and high resistances. Stellar Glimmer and Electro counter him, and Stellar-Conduct works best.
- **Emperor of Fire and Iron** is protected by horns charged with Pyro. Frequent Hydro quenches the horns and the Sternshield Crabs, while Pyro is useless here.
- **Radiant Moongecko** places an Anchor with a Geo ward and traps characters in a Cocoon. Bring Geo and a healer with a big burst heal.

From Menacing difficulty characters cannot be repeated, so prepare three different teams in advance. The page was put together before the cycle started, from the official announcement; we'll refine the teams after the first clears.

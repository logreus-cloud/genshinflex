### Public profiles

You can now share your profile. Every account has its own page at **genshinflex.com/u/name**. It shows your profile design (cover, background, framed avatar, effects and color), your title, "About me" and favorite characters.

- **The address** is set in your [profile](/en/profile/): "Customize profile → Basics". By default it looks like `user_1a2b3c4d`, so pick your own right away: 3 to 24 characters, Latin letters, digits, `_` and `-`.
- **The link** is copied with the "Copy link" button next to "Public page". In Discord and Telegram, the link preview shows the player's name and "About me".

### Privacy

The profile editor has a new **"Privacy"** tab. All personal data is hidden by default. You can open each block separately:

- **UID and showcase** — your UID with a copy button, Adventure Rank, World Level and the characters from your in-game showcase;
- **My characters** — your roster from the team checker;
- **Favorites** — characters and weapons marked with ☆;
- **Wishes** — a summary without the full history: total wishes, 5★ count, current pity and your latest 5★. If your UID is hidden, accounts are labeled "Account 1", "Account 2" instead of their numbers.

The same tab has the **"Show my profile to anyone with the link"** switch: turn it off and the whole profile page becomes unavailable. The settings only work while you're signed in.

### Ten new full guides

Full guides for **Skirk** and **Escoffier** (their rerun banner starts October 13) and **Diona**. Seven more for **Lohen, Prune, Varka, Durin, Jahoda, Dahlia and Furina**, with a breakdown of the Hexerei and Moonsign mechanics.

Each guide covers weapons and artifact sets with notes, target stats, talent priority, teams, rotation, mechanics, constellations and weaknesses. In all three languages.

### Fixes

- The menu button (☰) no longer stops working if the tab was open while the site was updated.
- The [artifact database](/en/artifacts/) now has icons and the 1-piece bonus for the four "Prayers" sets.

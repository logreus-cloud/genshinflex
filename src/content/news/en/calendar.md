Our first update since launch: there's now a **calendar**, picking characters and weapons is easier, and we fixed the bugs we found in the tools.

### Calendar

The [calendar](/en/calendar/) puts everything happening in the game on one timeline:

- character banners with the featured characters' icons and weapon banners, including past phases;
- events and versions, straight from the game's official announcements;
- Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater and Stygian Onslaught resets, including the expected next cycles;
- developer livestream announcements once they're out.

Zoom in to a week or out to a quarter, filter categories and jump to today. Click a bar to see its dates, artwork and a "time left" or "starts in" countdown. Times follow the server of your selected region. On phones the calendar opens as a list: "Ongoing", "Upcoming" and "Recently ended". The nearest events now also show up on the [home page](/en/).

### Easier character and weapon picking

In the [team checker](/en/tools/team-check/) and the [level-up calculator](/en/tools/calculator/), the plain list is replaced with a dropdown showing icons, rarity and element. Matches are highlighted, English names work too, and you can pick with the mouse or with the arrow keys and Enter.

### Fixes

**Team checker**

- A character can no longer end up in your list twice; old duplicates are merged automatically.
- Importing by UID no longer undoes clearing the list or overwrites edits you made while the showcase was loading.
- If the showcase has no artifacts or weapon, the empty slots are no longer filled with the recommended build.
- A team with an empty slot no longer gets "Ready for 9★": empty slots lower the score.
- Final stats are rated more fairly: zeros count, incomplete data no longer inflates the score, and an incomplete set isn't treated as a full one.
- The editor no longer accepts impossible values such as 2000% CRIT DMG.
- Keyboard selection is no longer lost when things switch or refresh.

**Home page**

- The background artwork no longer jumps when you switch search tabs.

If you spot a bug or an event is missing from the calendar, let us know via [feedback](/en/feedback/).

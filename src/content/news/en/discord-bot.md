### The bot on our server

[Our Discord server](https://discord.gg/rQ6pTDqdX9) now has the GenshinFlex bot. Start typing "/" in chat and it will suggest the commands. The command names are in Russian, since the server is Russian-speaking:

- **/гайд** (guide) — a character's weapons, artifacts, stats and teams. The name autocompletes as you type.
- **/профиль** (profile) — a player's public profile by username, or yours without one.
- **/баннеры** (banners) — current banners, time left and what comes next.
- **/гарант** (pity) — how many wishes are left to pity on each banner and whether your next 5★ is guaranteed. It is counted from your wish history on the site, and only you see the reply.

For /гарант and your own /профиль, Discord must be linked to your GenshinFlex account.

### Notifications in your DMs

In the profile editor, on the "Notifications" tab, you can turn on **forwarding to Discord**: the bot will send replies, mentions, friend requests and new messages to your DMs with a link to the site.

If notifications don't arrive, open the GenshinFlex server menu → "Privacy Settings" and turn on "Direct Messages": otherwise Discord won't let the bot message you.

### Roles for your account

Open the server menu → **"Linked Roles"** and connect GenshinFlex. Roles for a linked account, forum rating and guild membership are given automatically and update by themselves when your points or guild change.

### Linking Discord and Google

In [account settings](/en/account/) you can now link Discord or Google to an existing account, for example if you signed up with email, and unlink them. A Discord or Google account can be linked to only one GenshinFlex account.

### Compact mode for a second monitor

- [/overlay/](/en/overlay/) — character search and a guide in a narrow panel: weapons, artifacts, stats, talents and teams.
- [/overlay/banners/](/en/overlay/banners/) — current banners with a timer and your pity tracker.

Handy to keep in a separate window next to the game.

### Guides

- The **Faruzan** guide has been updated using Game8 and the official HoYoWiki recommendations: at C6 the best set is Tenacity of the Millelith, before C6 it's Viridescent Venerer. Added a 2 + 2 option, The Stringless and new teams.

For Halloween the site has a new look for one day. It's an event theme: on November 1 everything goes back to normal.

### What changed

- A dark header with a garland of pumpkins, ghosts and bats.
- A blood moon background on the [home page](/en/), crimson tabs and a pumpkin ornament under the banner.
- News and event cards in gothic frames, with gate arches around the carousels.
- A pumpkin in the logo and bats next to the title.
- An ornament in the footer instead of snow.

The theme also works in the site's light mode. All sections and features stay the same; only the look has changed.

Happy Halloween!

## Who is responsible
GenshinFlex is run by its owner as a personal, noncommercial fan project. It is not affiliated with HoYoverse. You can reach us through the feedback form, our Discord server, or an email address if one is shown below.

## Using the site without an account
You do not need to register. Your browser keeps your theme, server region, favorites, roster and calculator data, wish history and UID, guide drafts, recently viewed pages, and sign-in session (`gf:auth`) in localStorage. These stay on your device except in the cases below. You can clear them in your browser settings. We use no analytics, ad trackers, or tracking cookies.

## Game services
If you load a character showcase by UID, our proxy requests it from enka.network. Cloudflare caches the response by UID without linking it to you. If you import wish history, the link containing `authkey` passes through our proxy to HoYoverse; we neither store nor log it.

## Feedback and guide submissions
Cloudflare D1 stores the text, page, optional contact details, and a hash of your IP address for rate limiting. We do not store the IP itself. Feedback and submissions are forwarded to a private team channel on Discord. Bug reports and ideas go to public server forums without contact details or IP. Ask us through the form or Discord to remove your message.

## Optional account
Email sign-in uses your email and password; Supabase stores only a password hash. Discord or Google sign-in provides your email, name, and avatar; Telegram provides your ID, name, username, and photo. Your profile holds a username, display name, avatar, private game UID, region, language, bio, and profile visibility setting. When you are signed in, your "My profile" customization (nickname, bio, avatar, cover and background — including images you upload — colors, frame, effects and favorite characters), favorites, "my characters" list and showcase UID are stored in your account and synced across your devices. Profile customization is part of your public profile: other users will be able to see it if your profile is public. For signed-in users, wish history is stored only in the account — separately from the profile and privately — and is not kept in the browser; history already saved in the browser is moved to the account when you sign in and removed from the browser. Without an account, wish history stays only in your browser. We use this information to provide sign-in and account features, based on the consent you give at registration.

## Forum
Forum threads, posts, and reactions are public: anyone, including guests and search engines, can see them along with the author's nickname and avatar. We store the post text, when it was created and edited, its section, the thread language, and the post you reply to. If you delete a post or a moderator hides it, others no longer see it, but the text stays in the database and remains visible to moderators so they can handle reports and repeat violations. Only moderators see reports: we store who reported which post, the reason, the comment, and the decision. Forum bans are stored with their duration and reason. To prevent spam, the database checks posting frequency and account age. We do not store IP addresses for the forum separately; Supabase and Cloudflare keep technical logs for a limited time under their own policies. Please follow the [community rules](/en/rules/) when posting. The legal basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in moderating the site.

## Follows, friends, notifications, and private messages
**Connections.** We store who you follow, who follows you, friend requests and friendships, and who you have blocked. Follower, following, and friend counts are visible on your public profile; the lists themselves only if you allow it in your privacy settings. We never show anyone who has blocked them. To prevent spam, the database keeps a technical counter of actions (follows, requests, new conversations, and messages) and erases it after a day.

**Notifications.** We store notifications about replies, mentions, follows, friend requests, and messages: who they are from, what they are about, and whether you have read them. Read notifications older than 90 days are deleted automatically. You choose which notifications to receive in the profile editor. If you signed in with Telegram and turned on forwarding, our bot sends the notification to Telegram: the author's name, the thread title, the first 160 characters of the text, and a link. Such a message passes through Telegram's servers and is stored under Telegram's rules; you can turn forwarding off at any time.

**Private messages.** A conversation is visible only to its participants. We store the message text, the time it was sent, and read and deletion marks. You choose who can message you in your privacy settings: everyone, followers, or friends; blocked users cannot message you. If you delete a message, its text is erased from the database and the other person sees "Message deleted". Moderators do not read private messages. The exception is a report: if the other person reports a message, a moderator sees the copy of that message saved with the report and up to five messages before and after it to understand the context. The copy is kept with the report for as long as the report or the reporter's account exists, even if the author deleted the message. The legal basis is performance of a contract (running the features you use) and our legitimate interest in moderation.

**Guilds and ranking.** Guild membership is public: the guild tag and name appear next to your name and on the guild page in the member list. For the forum ranking we store which threads you opened (the thread and the day, without the time or IP address) and count points from reactions to your posts, replies in your threads, and views of your threads. Only public profiles appear in the overall ranking.

## Service providers
Supabase handles the database and sign-in on EU servers; Cloudflare hosts the site and API and provides Turnstile bot protection; Sanity manages site content without user data; Resend sends confirmation and password-reset emails; Discord receives feedback. Discord, Google, or Telegram are involved in sign-in only when you choose them. We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

## How long data is kept
Account data remains while your account exists and is erased when you delete it. Your forum reactions, reports, and ban are deleted with the account, while your threads and posts stay in discussions without an author, shown as "Deleted user", so other people's replies keep their context. To remove their text as well, delete your posts before deleting the account or ask us, and we will erase them from the database. Follows, friendships, blocks, notifications, reports, guild membership, and thread views are deleted with the account; if you owned a guild, it passes to its longest-standing member. Your private messages stay with the other person, shown from "Deleted user", because they are part of their conversation; you can delete their text beforehand, before deleting the account. Supabase backups may remain for a limited period under its policy. We keep feedback and submissions while they are useful for running the site and remove them on request.

## Your choices and rights
You can download your data or delete your account from the account page. The copy includes follows, friends, notifications, all your conversations in full, your guild, and thread views. You can change your profile, its appearance, and privacy settings in My profile, and edit or delete your posts on the forum. You can withdraw consent or complain to your country's data protection authority. GDPR applies in the EU.

## Age
You must be at least 13 to register. If your country requires a higher age to consent to data processing (up to 16 in the EU), you need parental consent.
